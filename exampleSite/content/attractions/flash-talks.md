Feynman once said, “If you cannot explain something in simple terms, you don’t understand it.” If you have a knack for breaking down complex theories into simple, relatable ideas, then Flash Talks are your perfect stage.

No slides. No jargon. No lengthy explanations.
Just you—your ideas, your creativity, and your voice.

In Flash Talks, you present your research using everyday language, supported by models, props, metaphors, or even parody songs and playful demonstrations. True to its name, a flash talk is quick, crisp, and captivating, lasting only a few minutes. It’s the ideal platform to 'flash' your communication skills, experiment with innovative storytelling and connect with the audience in a fun, refreshing and unforgettable day.
