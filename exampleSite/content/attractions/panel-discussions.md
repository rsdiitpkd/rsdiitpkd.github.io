Panel Discussions at RSD bring together diverse voices to explore some of today’s most relevant research challenges and opportunities. Featuring voices of expert professors from various department of the institute, the platform gives an opportunity to be aware of the research ecosystem and new frontiers in the field of research and technology.

The conversations are dynamic, interactive, and designed to spark new ideas. Whether you’re seeking clarity, inspiration, or a deeper understanding of key research themes, panel discussions offer a platform where complex issues are unpacked through meaningful, multi-dimensional discussions.
