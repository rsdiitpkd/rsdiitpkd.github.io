With 11 departments spanning diverse fields of science and engineering, IIT Palakkad offers a vibrant ecosystem of research and innovation. Research Scholars’ Day opens the doors of this ecosystem to external participants, giving them a unique opportunity to explore our academic units, specialized laboratories, and state-of-the-art facilities. This experience can become the starting point for future collaborations, the discovery of new experimental or testing methods, or even the spark for an entirely new research idea. Whether you are a research professional, a student, or simply an enthusiast, this is a chance to engage with cutting-edge research environments and connect with potential collaborators.
